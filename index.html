Built for the coast

Salt air, sea fog and storm season

Life on the Oregon Coast is hard on heating and cooling equipment. Here’s what we keep an eye on.

Salt-air corrosion

Salt spray works on outdoor coils, fins and fasteners. Regular inspection and cleaning as part of annual maintenance help catch corrosion early.

Damp-coast humidity

Months of mist and rain mean damp air indoors. A well-maintained heating system helps keep living spaces drier — and makes it easier to spot moisture and mold concerns early.

Storm outages

Winter storms can knock out power along Highway 101. Ask us how your gas fireplace, furnace or water heater behaves when the power goes out.

Heat pumps & the mild coast

The coast rarely gets extreme cold or heat, which suits heat pumps and ductless systems for efficient year-round comfort.

Rebates: Central Lincoln PUD lists residential rebates for air-source and ductless heat pumps. You may qualify — check eligibility with Central Lincoln PUD.
